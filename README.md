### About me

I'm an M.Sc. Computer Science student at **McGill University**, where my research sits at the intersection of **reinforcement learning, large language models, and robotics**. Alongside my studies, I'm the founder and CTO of **[Streamwise](https://streamwise.gg)** — an AI startup backed by McGill, Next AI, and Mila — where we're building a low-latency ASR + LLM system for real-time speech moderation in live streaming.

I'm interested in LLM reasoning and robustness, speech recognition, computer vision, and generative AI — and especially in **turning research ideas into real, shipped products**. When I'm not coding or training models, you'll probably find me bouldering, skiing, filming something, cooking, or sneaking in another business idea on the side.
